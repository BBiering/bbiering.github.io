Privacy policy

These are personal, single-user automation tools operated by the account owner. They are not distributed and have no other users.

The Google Drive integration accesses only the Drive of the account that authorized it, and only to create and update audio files and playlists that the operator asked for. No Google user data is collected, stored on a server, shared, sold, or used for advertising or training.

No third-party analytics, cookies, or tracking are used. Data is never transferred to third parties except the storage providers the operator explicitly configured.
